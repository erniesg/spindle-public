Earned leisure

Sign in to see your earned leisure.

Off unless you turn it on. Then each second of observed time that you yourself labelled “learning” (your correction or saved rule) earns one second of leisure, counted over the last 24 hours. It is not proof of learning; model guesses, confidence and finished timers never count. Leisure is spent as a 30–1800 second pause of a Chrome site block.

This access is separate from timer access, lasts only while this app runs, and ends when the profile is re-paired or revoked, the account changes or signs out, or the app quits. Chrome sends only the blocked site name, its policy version and the seconds you choose; it never receives your account sign-in.
